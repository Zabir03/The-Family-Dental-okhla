About the clinic

Dental care you can trust

The Family Dental is a neighbourhood dental clinic in Abul Fazal Enclave, Jamia Nagar, looking after everyday dental health for individuals and families across Okhla and nearby areas of New Delhi.

Care here is unhurried. We examine properly, explain what we find in plain language, and go through the options with you before anything begins — so you always know what is being done and why.

  • Routine and preventive dental care for all ages, from children to older adults.
  • Late evening appointments on most days, so treatment fits around work and school.
  • Easy to reach from Jamia Nagar, Abul Fazal Enclave, Shaheen Bagh and Okhla.

Dental treatments

Treatments we look after

General and family dentistry under one roof. Tap any treatment to ask about it in the appointment form.

  • General dentistry

    Everyday dental care — examination, advice and treatment for common tooth and gum problems.

  • Dental check-ups

    A routine look at teeth, gums and bite to catch small problems before they turn painful.

  • Teeth cleaning

    Scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar build-up along the gum line.

  • Root canal treatment

    Treatment for an infected or badly decayed tooth, aimed at saving the natural tooth.

  • Dental fillings

    Cavities cleaned and filled to restore the shape and function of the tooth.

  • Tooth extraction

    Removal of a tooth that cannot be saved, including wisdom tooth assessment.

  • Teeth whitening

    Professional whitening to lighten stains and discolouration on natural teeth.

  • Crowns & bridges

    Caps and fixed replacements that rebuild broken, weak or missing teeth.

  • Dental implants

    A fixed option for replacing a missing tooth, starting with a suitability assessment.

  • Orthodontic consultation

    An assessment of crowded or uneven teeth, with guidance on the alignment options available.

  • Gum care

    Treatment and advice for bleeding, swollen or receding gums and related discomfort.

  • Children's dental care

    Gentle check-ups and treatment for children, at a pace that keeps them comfortable.

Treatment details and availability are confirmed at the clinic. Call 079826 97125 if you are unsure which one you need.
